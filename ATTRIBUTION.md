# Attribution — who built what

The source of truth for the ledger rendered on
[`root-system.html`](root-system.html), the hidden colophon reached through the
keyhole in the hub's hero.

**Rules for this file.** Two kinds of evidence count: a signature in the
piece's own source, and Tasha's own recollection recorded here. Nothing is
inferred from style, folder name, or adjacency. Where nobody can say, the entry
stays `unrecorded` and the page prints it that way — an honest blank beats a
confident guess.

**Status: 27 of 27 attributed.** No blanks remain.

**What this file does not track.** Every row records who *built* a piece, not
who has edited it since. Nearly all of them have had hand passes afterwards —
colours, wording, fonts, particle fields, sound, physics constants — made in
increments too small and too frequent to have been logged, and largely not
recoverable now. Treat a build credit as an origin, not a claim about the
current state of the file. Anything that later turns out to matter (a rename, a
behaviour change, a piece that became a different thing) belongs in the
accidents section of `root-system.html` rather than in these tables.

## Two naming things worth getting right

**Codex and Antigravity are tools, not models.** Codex is the harness OpenAI
models arrive through (GPT-5.5, GPT-5.6-Sol); Antigravity is the same
arrangement for Gemini. This is why the fidget hub's section heading says
"Codex" while the toy inside signs itself "OpenAI GPT-5.5" — both are correct,
and the ledger now credits them as `model, via tool`.

**Mistral appears twice under two names.** As "Vibe" on the fidget hub
(Emoji Popcorn), and as Le Chat, pre-rebrand, on MOM.SYS. Earlier versions of
this file wrongly warned against treating Le Chat as a builder — that came from
Le Chat appearing as a *character* in the Bake-Off transcript. It is both: a
character there, a real builder on MOM.SYS.

# 🗝 Rosetta's Garden of Keys

Every door here has its own key. Wander in.

Five small worlds, each grown from its own soil — a fidget garden, a deck that won't sit still, a spring older than any one tradition, an arcade that shouldn't exist yet, and a map of the galaxy you happen to be standing in.

**[Enter the garden →](https://rosettaskeys.github.io/playground/index.html)**

## The five doors

- **[Fidget Toys](https://rosettaskeys.github.io/playground/fidgetToy/fidgetToy-index.html)** — Live sensory toys in code. Touch, drag, press, and linger. Small loops for hands, eyes, and attention.
- **[Deck of Cards](https://rosettaskeys.github.io/playground/ecards/card-index.html)** — Live motion for occasions. A descent into wonderland, occasion by occasion.
- **[Spring of Ages](https://sites.google.com/view/springofages/home)** — A celebration of cultures. Easter across cultures, across time. Joy older than any one tradition.
- **[M1SF1RE ARC▓DE](https://rosettaskeys.github.io/playground/games/games-index.html)** — Games that chose chaos. Do not adjust your set.
- **[The Long Field](https://rosettaskeys.github.io/playground/milkyWay/milky-way-atlas.html)** — An atlas in two scales. Forty points of interest across the Milky Way, thirty more inside the Solar System, and a real publication behind every claim. ([sources](milkyWay/SOURCES.md))

## What this actually is

Plain HTML/CSS/JS. No framework, no build step, no bundler. Each piece is its own file — clone it, open it, poke at it. Nothing here needs a server, a package manager, or an explanation.

## Grown, not built

Everything here started as something small and a little strange — a toy for restless hands, a deck that refuses to hold still, a story about spring, an arcade that got out of hand. Some of it is useful. All of it is meant to be poked at.

Nothing here is finished. That's the point of a garden.
